What each one is

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, so a day-first tenant’s dates come out with day and month swapped. App, email and booking-v2 forms, every tenant on file.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. Only the booking-v3 form, so fixing the parser does not fix it.
  • one-day: the date moves by one day, not a swap. One meeting showed as Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto: a time zone bug. The desk had already read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) but filed them under the parser by tenant.

How it was counted

  • The export pages overlap, so the same report can appear twice. Counted once by id.
  • Cause comes from the tenant, its locale and its booking form (causes.ts), not from intake’s tag. The tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser, and it often disagrees with the tenant.
  • Every body with two dates was checked: the parser and hint reports swap day and month; none of them is a one-day move.

Lessons

  • A matching subject line says nothing about cause. Count by cause before marking a big ticket known.
  • Read the bodies of a sample before the ticket is closed as one issue.
